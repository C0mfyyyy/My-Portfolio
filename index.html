01 AI-Assisted Trading System In progress

Algorithmic Trading Bot

Personal project — current build

I'm building a trading bot that follows fixed rules instead of emotions. The current version is a team of five rule-based day-trading bots for US stock indices: each one watches the market from the 09:30 open, trades only when its rules fire, and closes every position before the 16:00 close, so nothing is held overnight.

How I'm testing it

The strategies were tuned on 2005–2012 data, then tested on years they had never seen, with trading costs taken off every trade. A strategy only passed if it stayed profitable on the unseen years and survived doubled costs. Results replay on a 3D "trading floor" where you can watch each bot trade day by day.

Where it's heading

Alongside it I'm simulating a multi-agent engine for gold (XAUUSD), where separate AI agents handle signals, risk and execution, and an MQL5 Expert Advisor for MetaTrader 5. The goal is to learn the full pipeline, from idea to backtest to live paper trading.

Key Features

5 Rule-Based Bots Out-of-Sample Testing Costs Included No Overnight Risk 3D Replay (Three.js) Multi-Agent Engine MQL5 Expert Advisor

02 AI-Powered Study App Live

Revision Desk

Personal project — built for my own diploma modules

Revision Desk is the study app I built to revise for my own modules. It turns lecture slides into short lessons, tap-to-reveal concept cards, flashcards and quick multiple-choice checks, all organised by module and test, with a dashboard that shows how ready I am for each upcoming test.

How AI fits in

I use AI to pull the key points, flashcards and practice questions out of my slides, then review and correct them before they go into the app. Each wrong answer comes with an explanation, so the quiz teaches as well as tests. It currently covers modules like IT Governance and Responsible AI.

What's next

Turning it into a standalone web app with sign-in and per-user data, and possibly releasing it on the Play Store and App Store later.
